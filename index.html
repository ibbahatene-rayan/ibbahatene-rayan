Topological Analysis of Vascular Networks

Research Project (TIPE)

Technologies: Python, Graph Theory, NetworkX, NumPy, Linear Algebra

  • Objective: Analyzed the topological robustness of vascular transport networks under localized damage using the 2025–2026 national research theme "Cycles et Boucles".
  • Methodology: Formulated Laplacian matrices and calculated Fiedler algebraic connectivity vectors to evaluate structural resilience. Modeled hydraulic pressure linear systems to analyze fluid dynamics under physical bottlenecks.
  • Results: Quantified the vital role of closed loop topographies in preventing transport failure during vascular occlusion.
